# Commercial licensing

FabricatorStudio is free for small studios.

If your organization made less than 2 million USD in gross revenue last tax year, you do
not need a license and you do not need to contact anyone. Use it in production, ship games
with it, use it on client work. It is yours, and it stays yours.

Personal use, learning, and portfolio work are always free, whatever you do for a day job.

Above 2 million USD in annual revenue, a commercial license is required.

## Pricing

One flat license for your whole studio, per year. Not per seat, and never a percentage of
what you make. Nothing to count, nothing to activate, no license server, no phoning home.

| Your annual revenue | Per year (USD) |
|---|---|
| Under 2 million | Free |
| 2 to 5 million | 1,000 |
| 5 to 15 million | 5,000 |
| 15 to 50 million | 15,000 |
| 50 to 250 million | 50,000 |
| 250 million to 1 billion | 150,000 |
| Over 1 billion | From 300,000, let us talk |

Crossing 2 million costs you 1,000 a year, which is less than a single Maya seat. That is
on purpose. You should not get punished for a good year.

## Why it works this way

These tools were built by one technical artist to give small teams the character pipeline
that large studios build in house.

If you cannot afford to hire a technical artist, you get the whole toolset free,
permanently. If you can afford one, you can pay for a license. That revenue is what keeps
the tools free for everyone else.

For comparison: Epic charges non-game companies over 1 million in revenue 1,850 USD per
seat per year for Unreal Engine. Our line is twice that, and above it you pay once for the
entire studio instead of once per person.

## Getting a license

Email licensing@fabricator.studio with your company name and revenue band. Licenses are
annual. No sales call required unless you want one.

## Questions

**We are a small studio owned by a large publisher.** The threshold counts your parent and
sibling companies. If a large company controls your studio, you are over the line.

**We spun out of a big company and we are independent now.** Then you are independent, and
only your own revenue counts. The test is who controls you today, not who used to.

**I am an individual contractor working at a large studio.** Your own learning, portfolio,
and personal work is free, always. Work you do for the large studio is use for their
benefit, so they need the license, not you. Point them here.

**What about the Advanced Ribbon Modules?** Those are a separate paid product for everyone,
at any size. A commercial license covers the free toolset for a large organization; it does
not include the Ribbon Modules, and buying the Ribbon Modules does not remove the need for
a commercial license if you are over the threshold.

**What happens to old versions?** Every release becomes open source under the GPL four
years after it ships. That is built into the license and cannot be revoked.

**Is this open source?** No, and we will not call it that. The source is public and you can
read and modify every line. It is not an OSI approved license, because those licenses
cannot ask a large company to pay.
