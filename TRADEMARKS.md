- Registering the marks, or confusingly similar marks or domain names, anywhere.

You may fork and modify the code under the terms of the Business Source License 1.1. If you distribute a modified version, give it your own distinct name and make clear it is not the official FabricatorStudio. You may still state that it is "based on FabricatorStudio".
